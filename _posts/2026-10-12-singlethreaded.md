## Prolog
A lot of people nowadays take the position in the debate about life creation, that we are a straighforward simulation.
The might be right, or not, who knows. But if we will for a minute assume that this theory is true, how could we be implemented?
I am heading into questions like how would the outer universe interact with us or if we are real time simlation or not. What I am point at is that for anyone who has done atleast some programming, it is clear that we (our brains) in this world model are surely single-threaded.

Therefore, it would indeed be strange if our brains could multi-task. It would certainly be strange and partly in contradiction with the world we know.
You might argue that humans have to have more "threads". We are indeed talking, and moving at the same time right. And this actions, even though not always, are in sync.

And that I would shortly agree with, however my focus in on the brain itself. Are you able to think and reason about what will you have for dinner while thinking about which arguments should your endpoint have? 
If you are not Sherlock Holmes, you are probably not. And this is where comes my point about all the agentic swarm workflow and 20 different session working at the same time comes in.

## Bottleneck to quality and happiness?
I had this thought, that it would be super cool to implement something like a software factory. Once you are assigned a ticket, you will just slightly tweak the specification and dispatch the worker. Then you will review the work and open a PR.
And this works, I already tested it. It actually works brilliantly, for the tasks that I do not care about at all. For the tasks, where I can just look at the end result and it is immidietly clear which changes where made.

But after dispatching 5 workers at the same time on bigger problem, I realized I am the bottleneck. Constant context switching is drastically decreasing my focus, creativity, performance
I feel like our brain was not made for this constant context switching and our human brains might become the bottleneck. 

Unless you create something that can review like human and at the same time precisly know what you imagined on the output after the workers job, even though you might not even thought about it, because when dispatching the worker, you were context switching to another worker :).


## Epilog
I feel like the constant noise around you is hurting, everything about how your brain was supossed to work. 
Let's decrease the noise, improve our memory, cognitive functions and overall path to happiness. :)
